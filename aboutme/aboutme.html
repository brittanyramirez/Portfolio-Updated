ABOUT ME

A little more about who I am. Beyond the code.

Hi my name is Brittany Ramirez I'm a 23 year old aspiring software engineer with a background in medical assisting and a passion for creating thoughtful, user centered digital experiences. As a proud Salvadorian and a mother to a one year old daughter, my journey into tech has been shaped by resilience, adaptability, and a strong sense of purpose.

Before transitioning into software development, I worked in healthcare, where attention to detail, clear communication, and empathy were essential every day. That experience continues to influence how I approach technology with a focus on accessibility, problem solving, and building solutions that genuinely support people. I'm now fully immersed in developing my technical skill set while continuing to grow professionally, excited to build meaningful applications that blend creativity, functionality, and impact.
